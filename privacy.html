Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-23

“jitsuha uploader” is a private tool operated by the YouTube channel ジツハ構造 (@jitsuhakouzou). It is used only by the channel’s operator to upload the channel’s own videos to YouTube.

Data the app accesses

With the operator’s consent, the app uses the YouTube Data API scope youtube.upload to upload videos and set their title, description and publish time. It does not read, collect or store any data about other users.

Storage

The OAuth token is stored only on the operator’s own computer. No data is sent to any server other than Google’s APIs, and nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
